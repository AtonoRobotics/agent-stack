# ARRI Alexa Mini

## Specifications
- Body Weight: 2.3 kg (body only)
- Dimensions: 130 x 114 x 58 mm (H x W x D)
- Sensor: ALEV III CMOS, Super 35
- Resolution: Up to 3.4K (3424 x 2202)
- Dynamic Range: 14+ stops
- Lens Mount: PL mount (54mm flange depth)
- Recording: ProRes 4444 XQ, ARRIRAW
- Power: 10.5-34V DC, ~65W typical

## Mount Configuration
- Mounted via custom bracket to Dobot CR10 flange
- PL mount faces forward (end-effector Z-axis)
- Camera body center of mass: [0, -0.02, 0.06] m from mount plate
- Total with lens: ~3.9 kg (body + lens)

## Integration Notes
- Video output: SDI (BNC)
- Control: WiFi (ARRI Camera Companion app)
- Timecode: Via BNC input
- Power from robot arm: 24V through slip ring
