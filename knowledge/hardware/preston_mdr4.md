# Preston MDR4

## Specifications
- Type: Wireless Lens Motor Driver
- Weight: 0.4 kg (receiver unit only)
- Motors Installed: Focus + Iris (2 channels active)
- Motor Type: Digital microforce motors
- Channels: 4 (Focus, Iris, Zoom, Aux)
- Control Range: Wireless up to 300m line of sight
- Protocol: Preston proprietary 900MHz/2.4GHz
- Power: 12-24V DC, ~8W

## Current Configuration (Alpha CR10)
- Focus motor: Mounted on Zeiss Master Prime focus ring
- Iris motor: Mounted on Zeiss Master Prime iris ring
- Zoom motor: Not installed (prime lens)
- Aux motor: Not installed
- Hand unit: Preston HU4 (operator-held)

## Mounting
- Receiver mounted on camera right side
- Center of mass offset from camera mount: X:0mm Y:+35mm Z:0mm
- Connected to robot 24V power via accessory port

## Integration Notes
- FIZ data available via serial output (RS-422)
- Lens metadata (focus distance, T-stop) readable for digital twin
- Motor positions can be recorded for shot playback
- Compatible with all PL-mount cine lenses
