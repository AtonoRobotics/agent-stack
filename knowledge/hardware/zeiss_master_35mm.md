# Zeiss Master Prime 35mm

## Specifications
- Focal Length: 35 mm
- Maximum Aperture: T1.3
- Close Focus: 0.35 m (14")
- Weight: 1.6 kg
- Length: 128 mm
- Front Diameter: 95 mm (clip-on)
- Mount: PL mount
- Image Circle: Covers Super 35

## Optical Characteristics
- Minimal breathing
- Consistent T-stop across focus range
- Color matched to Master Prime family
- Focus rotation: 300deg

## Integration Notes
- Focus/Iris/Zoom controlled by Preston MDR4
- Focus gear: 0.8 module standard cine gear
- Iris gear: 0.8 module
- Calibrated focus marks stored in Preston HU4
